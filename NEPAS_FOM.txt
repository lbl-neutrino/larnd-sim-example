Mean	12811.81
stdev	12.51

The FOM equation is defined as
Total number of GPU-node-hours needed to process N events (feedback: events/node hour)So basically
node-hr/event = (12811.81/3600)/13 = 0.27 node-hr/event
Then for N events:
total node-hr≈0.27 N
--
So for the GPU-node-hours
We just need to divide by a further factor of 4 to account for the multiple GPUs on each node.
So we have 0.27 GPU-hr/event = 0.0675 node-hr/event (Thanks, Matt).
